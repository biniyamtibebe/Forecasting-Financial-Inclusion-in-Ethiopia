# Forecasting Financial Inclusion in Ethiopia – Key Insights

## 1. Current State
Ethiopia’s account ownership increased rapidly between 2011 and 2021,
but growth slowed sharply after 2021 despite massive mobile money expansion.

## 2. Why Growth Slowed
- Most mobile money users already had bank accounts
- P2P transfers dominate, limiting new formal adoption
- Structural constraints (ID, income, trust)

## 3. Role of Key Events
- Telebirr drove usage more than access
- Safaricom increased competition but not immediate inclusion
- M-Pesa shows stronger usage effects than access effects

## 4. Forecasts (2025–2027)
Baseline scenario suggests:
- Account ownership reaches ~53–55% by 2027
- Digital payment usage grows faster than access

Optimistic scenarios require:
- Merchant acceptance growth
- Government payment digitization

## 5. Policy Implications
Access alone is no longer the bottleneck.
Usage depth and merchant ecosystems matter more.
